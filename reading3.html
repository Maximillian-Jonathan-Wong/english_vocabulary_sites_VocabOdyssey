Educating Psyche

Suggestopedia, paraconscious memory, music-assisted learning, and the psychology of suggestion.

1. Radical adjective
/ˈræd.ɪ.kəl/
Representing complete or extreme change from traditional ideas or methods.
Educating Psyche by Bernie Neville is a book which looks at radical new approaches to learning, describing the effects of emotion, imagination and the unconscious on learning.
Source: IELTS Educating Psyche
2. Durable adjective
/ˈdjʊə.rə.bəl/
Able to last or remain strong in memory for a long period of time.
Lozanov's instructional technique is based on the evidence that the connections made in the brain through unconscious processing are more durable than those made through conscious processing.
Source: IELTS Educating Psyche
3. Peripherally adverb
/pəˈrɪf.ər.əl.i/
At the outer edge of conscious attention rather than as the central focus.
Besides the laboratory evidence for this, we know from our experience that we often remember what we have perceived peripherally, long after we have forgotten what we set out to learn.
Source: IELTS Educating Psyche
4. Elusive adjective
/ɪˈluː.sɪv/
Difficult to recall, find, capture, or define clearly.
Even if these peripheral details are a bit elusive, they come back readily in hypnosis or when we relive the event imaginatively, as in psychodrama.
Source: IELTS Educating Psyche
5. Counterproductive adjective
/ˌkaʊn.tə.prəˈdʌk.tɪv/

6. Solemnly adverb
/ˈsɒl.əm.li/
